What the arms can and cannot do

No YAM joint spins all the way round. Each joint stops at a hard limit (from i2rt's own yam.urdf). Plan every move so no joint has to go near its end.

Base turn (joint 1)−150° to +179°
Work in front of the arm, within about 60° of straight ahead. Never reach behind the base.
Shoulder (joint 2)0° to 209°
Bends one way only. Objects closer than about 15 cm to the base are awkward to reach.
Elbow (joint 3)0° to 179°
Bends one way only, never backwards. Keep it bent; a fully straight arm is at its reach limit.
Wrist bends (joints 4, 5)−90° to +90°
At most a right angle each way. Pointing the fingers straight down is fine; pointing them back at the arm is not.
Gripper twist (joint 6)−120° to +120°
240° in total, no continuous spin. Keep twists under 90° and never flip the card over.
Gripper96 mm opening
Grip the 14 mm handle or the card's edge, never the flat face. Card ≈ 15 g; payload 2 kg.
Reach box (recording)5–60 cm forward
±45 cm sideways, 0–55 cm high from the base. Teleop clamps the tool inside this box.
Leader-arm recordingright arm only
You move the left arm by hand and the right arm copies it. Only the right arm does the task in recordings; the left stays still.

Skill 1 · Draw the card

"pick up the black card and hold it in the air"
Right arm10–20 s per take50+ takesSame words Ramsha is recording with
#MoveTimeGripperHow to do it within the limits
1Start at home1 sopenStart the take with the arm at home and still. Every take starts from the same pose.
2Reach above the card3–4 sopenTurn the base toward the card (under 60°) and stretch forward. Stop about 15 cm above the card with the fingers pointing straight down.
3Line up1–2 sfully openTwist the gripper so the fingers sit either side of the handle (or the card's short edge). Twist under 90°.
4Come straight down1–2 sfully openLower vertically, slowing for the last 3 cm, until the fingertips are below the top of the handle.
5Close all the way1 sclosed hardSqueeze the gripper fully every time. Do not stop half-closed. The model copies how far you close.
6Pause0.5 sclosed hardStay still for half a second before lifting. This teaches "grip first, then move".
7Lift straight up2 sclosed hardRaise 15–20 cm without swinging sideways. Keep the card upright.
8Hold in the air2–3 sclosed hardStay still, card visible to the overhead camera. Stop the take while still holding.

Skill 2 · Place it in the stand

"place the card you are holding in the stand"
Right arm10–15 s per take30+ takesStart each take holding the card
#MoveTimeGripperHow to do it within the limits
1Start holding the card1 sclosed hardBegin where skill 1 ended: card gripped, held in the air. Pick it up by hand-guiding first, then start the take.
2Move above the stand3 sclosed hardCarry the card level to about 10 cm above the stand's slot. Base turn stays small.
3Line up with the slot1–2 sclosed hardTwist the gripper so the card is parallel to the slot, face toward the guest. Twist under 90°.
4Lower into the slot2 sclosed hardGo straight down until the card's bottom edge sits in the slot.
5Let go1 sfully openOpen the gripper fully and wait a moment so the card settles.
6Lift away1 sopenGo straight up 10 cm so the fingers do not knock the card.
7Return home3 sopenBack to the same home pose, then stop the take.

Rules for every take

  • Use the exact prompt words above for every take of that skill, and the same words live.
  • Move smoothly and a little slower than feels natural; Dream-1 plays plans back at about two thirds of recorded speed anyway.
  • Move the card a few centimetres around its spot between takes, so the model learns to find it.
  • Same lighting, same table, same stand position. Nobody's hands in the cameras.
  • Throw away any take with a fumble, a half-closed grip or a bump straight away.
  • About 1 in 10 takes may show a recovery: miss, open, line up again, grip. That teaches the model to retry.
  • Fix the right wrist camera before relying on live runs: Dream-1 currently stretches a 640×480 picture to 16:9.

Train, then run it

  1. Record skill 1 (50+) and skill 2 (30+) into one dataset folder. One fine-tune learns both prompts.
  2. Run tools/finetune.py --check (free) on the folder.
  3. Upload and queue 500 steps ($100) by about 10 pm. Leave credit above the $100 so the arms can keep running Dream-1 while it trains.
  4. When the model is ready, the page runs skill 1, then skill 2, with --checkpoint and --action-space joint.
  5. Load the new model once without moving the arms, then do a live test with someone at the emergency stop.
If time runs short, record skill 1 only. The Fortune Teller can reveal the card while the arm holds it in the air.
