    What the model taught us

    • Pointing straight down, the wrist (joint 4, ±90°) cannot hold the grasp point much above 15 cm. Higher up, tilt the gripper back about 35°, as the arm does here.
    • The gripper twist (joint 6) stops at ±120°. With the card's face toward the guest at the card spot it would sit at −109°, 11° from the stop. So the card stands sideways in its rack and the arm turns it to face the guest at the stand.
    • Shoulder and elbow bend one way only; keep the card spot and stand 25–40 cm in front of the base.
    • In this motion no joint comes closer than 10° to a stop.

    Recording it

    • Hand-guide the left (leader) arm through these moves; the right arm copies them.
    • Start every take at home, end skill 1 holding the card in the air, end skill 2 back at home.
    • Close fully on the handle every time and pause half a second before lifting.
    • Shift the card a few centimetres between takes. 50+ takes of skill 1, 30+ of skill 2.
    • Check that takes are being saved: the dataset folder should grow after each one.
