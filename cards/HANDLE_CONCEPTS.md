# Card handle concepts (v3): 10 options

Goal: replace the plain grey block on top of the card with something that looks like part of a fortune card, while keeping what made v1 grippable.

## What worked (Nov 7, ~2 pm)

Base Dream-1 on NUCK-8 (YAM arms, i2rt `linear_4310` parallel gripper) picked card v1 up by its top handle: a 30 × 18 × 14 mm block with two flat parallel grip faces 14 mm apart, flush with the card back and 10 mm proud of the front.

## Constraints every concept must keep

- Card body 63 × 88 × 4 mm (poker size), printed flat on its back, PLA, **no supports**.
- Two **flat, parallel grip faces** at the top, at least ~20 mm wide and ~12 mm tall, 10 to 16 mm apart (the v1 handle that worked: 30 × 18, 14 mm apart).
- Fits the deck tray (cards lean 15°, 14.4 mm pitch) and the reveal stand (6 mm slot, 40 mm funnel).
- Readable as one object by the overhead camera; no thin fragile spikes the gripper could snap.

## The 10 concepts

1. **Crescent moon crest**: the handle's outline is a crescent moon; the grip faces are the flat front and back of the crest (12 mm thick), so it reads as a moon sitting on the card.
2. **Sun crown**: a half-sun with short blunt rays rising from the top edge; the central disc is 14 mm thick (grip), rays 4 mm thick and decorative.
3. **Ornate header bar**: the whole top edge thickens into a 63 mm wide, 14 mm tall, 12 mm thick header with the card name engraved; the gripper can close anywhere along 63 mm.
4. **Gothic arch tab**: a pointed-arch tab (cathedral window) 32 mm wide, 12 mm thick, with a recessed rose pattern on the front.
5. **Crystal gem**: a faceted gem (hexagonal prism standing on the top edge); two opposite facets are the parallel grip faces.
6. **Wax seal medallion**: a round 30 mm disc standing on the top edge, 12 mm thick, embossed with the card's symbol like a wax seal.
7. **All-seeing eye triangle**: an equilateral triangle tab (34 mm base) with an engraved eye, 12 mm thick, blunt tip.
8. **Thick tarot tile**: no handle at all: the whole card is a 10 mm thick tile with chamfered edges; the gripper closes on the card's top 18 mm (front and back faces).
9. **Side notches (no protrusion)**: the card is grabbed across its 63 mm width by its two side edges, each with a V notch near the top that centres the fingers; the top stays flat.
10. **Twisted key bow**: the handle is the bow of an antique key (a ring with a flat 12 mm thick body and a filled centre), as if the card were a key to the future.
